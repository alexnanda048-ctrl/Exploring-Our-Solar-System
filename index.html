About

The purpose of this site is to teach you about the solar system, mainly the 8 planets that inhabit it. All 8 of these planets orbit our one sun. Solar systems are comprised of all objects held by a stars gravity, in our case the sun. Additionally it is worth noting that though we will be covering planets, many other objects can make up a solar system like meteors, comets, dwarf planets, even small debris and man made objects

About Mercury:

  • Moons: 0
  • Type: Terrestrial (solid)
  • The smallest planet.
  • Closest planet to the sun.

About Venus:

  • Moons: 0
  • Type: Terrestrial (solid)
  • The hottest planet.
  • Clouds made of corrosive sulfuric acid.

About Earth:

  • Moons: 1
  • Type: Terrestrial (solid)
  • Only known planet with life.

About Mars:

  • Moons: 2
  • Type: Terrestrial (solid)
  • We have had many robots explore it.
  • Has extreme dust storms.

About Jupiter:

  • Moons: 95
  • Type: Gas Giant (no solid surface, made of Helium and Hydrogen)
  • The largest planet, more than double the mass of the other planets in the solar system combined.
  • Jupiter is plagued by storms and constant hurricanes that can last up to a century.

About Saturn:

  • Moons: 146
  • Type: Gas Giant (no solid surface, made of Helium and Hydrogen)
  • Famous for its rings.

About Uranus:

  • Moons: 28
  • Type: Ice Giant (no solid surface, made of ice, rock, water, and gases)
  • First planet discovered by a telescope.
  • Rotates has an axial tilt of 98 degrees, meaning it rotates on what would be its side.

About Neptune:

  • Moons: 16
  • Type: Ice Giant (no solid surface, made of ice, rock, water, and gases)
  • Windiest planet.
  • Discovered using math.
  • Farthest from the sun.
